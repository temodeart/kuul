Kuul Privacy Policy

The Mongolian version is the official one. If the two differ, the Mongolian text applies.

This policy explains what personal data Kuul collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It follows Mongolia's Law on Personal Data Protection (2021).

1. Who is responsible

Temuujin Batbold, an individual in Ulaanbaatar, runs Kuul and decides how your data is used, so is responsible for it. For anything about your data, email hello@kuul.mn.

3. Why we can use it: your consent

3.1. We use your data because you consent to it. We ask separately for each purpose, and every box starts unticked:

  • Core (required for an account): your account, timetable, friends, chats and campus features, as described in this policy.
  • Data stored abroad (required while our server is outside Mongolia): see section 7.
  • A parent's or guardian's agreement (required if you are 16 or 17): see section 11.
  • Push notifications (optional).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (optional). We don't collect any today. If we start, we'll collect only counts of actions, never message content.

3.2. You can withdraw any consent in Settings. It stops future use and doesn't undo use before that. Withdrawing core consent means deleting your account, because Kuul can't work without it.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't use your messages for ads.
  • We don't ask for your school portal password.
  • We don't collect ID cards, selfies or any biometric data.
  • We don't track where you go, and we remove location data from the photos you upload.
  • Schools and partners only ever get totals that can't identify anyone.

7.3. Our agreements with providers limit them to the purpose above and forbid them from using your data for themselves. We remain responsible to you for what they do with it.

8. How long we keep data

8.1. The table in section 2 lists how long we keep each kind of data.

8.2. When you delete your account, your name, bio and photo are removed at once and you're signed out everywhere. After 30 days we delete the account and everything tied to it: your profile, timetable, friends, blocks, chat memberships, consents, notifications, verification records, uploaded files and lost and found posts.

8.3. Some things stay without your name, because they're part of other people's records or we need them for safety:

  • messages you sent to others, shown as sent by "Deleted user"
  • alerts you posted
  • reports you filed
  • logs of staff actions

8.4. Every night we back up the database and uploaded photos, encrypted, to separate storage at our cloud provider in Japan. Backups are kept 30 days, so anything deleted is gone from them within 30 days after that.

9. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • There are no passwords to steal. Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes, allow 3 tries, and are stored only as a hash.
  • Sessions rotate. If an old session key is reused, we sign that session out on every device.
  • On your phone, Kuul keeps your session in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).
  • Staff access depends on their role, and we log what they do.
  • Rate limits slow down anyone trying to guess codes or scrape data.

If personal data leaks, we tell the people affected and the National Human Rights Commission as the law requires. Our own target is to tell you within 72 hours of finding out.

10. Your rights

You can:

  • Ask what data we hold about you and who we shared it with.
  • Get a copy of your data as a JSON file, free, within 7 days.
  • Correct your data. You can edit your name and bio in the app. For anything else, such as your school, email us.
  • Delete messages and posts, or your whole account (Settings → Delete account).
  • Withdraw a consent in Settings.
  • Ask a person to review an automated decision. The keyword filter can hold a message automatically, but a person reviews it, and only a person can suspend an account.
  • Complain to the National Human Rights Commission of Mongolia, or go to court.

Email hello@kuul.mn for anything that isn't in the app. We reply within 10 working days.

11. Age limit

11.1. You must be at least 16 to use Kuul. We ask for your birth date at sign-up and turn away anyone younger. Your birth date is never shown to anyone.

11.2. If you are 16 or 17, show this policy and the Terms to a parent or guardian. If they agree, tick the box that says so on the consent screen. Users under 18 are matched by shake-to-befriend and friend suggestions only with other users under 18, and adults can find them in people search only if they go to the same school.

11.3. A parent or guardian can withdraw their agreement at hello@kuul.mn; we then close the account. If you think someone under 16 is using Kuul, email us and we will close the account and delete its data.

12. Changes to this policy

We'll tell you in the app at least 14 days before a change takes effect. If we want to use your data in a new way or share it with a new provider, we ask for your consent first.
